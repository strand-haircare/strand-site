Why STRAND

Made by a woman who relaxes.

I've relaxed my hair since I was about ten. Even with the budget to try everything, I couldn't find products built for it.

I went through the same cycle over and over. Fresh touch-up, then weeks of guessing. In its more fragile state, my hair needed care I didn't know how to give it, with products that weren't made for it. And it would break off.

For years, I didn't realize relaxed hair could be long and thriving. Once I saw that it could, I wanted every woman who relaxes to have the routine I never had.

The salon is great. But most of your hair's life happens at home.
