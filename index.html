The problem

She's settling for products not made for her. Or building a routine from ten brands.

Haircare moved on to curls. Women who relax or chemically straighten were left with old drugstore formulas, heavy oils, and salon products meant for someone else's hair. So she mixes and matches, and hopes it holds until her next touch-up.

Every strand holds two textures. Care for both.
