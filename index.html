Why start with oil

Your ends need a little extra.

Straightening changes the outer surface of your hair. Over time, ends can feel drier and rougher than the rest. A light oil is the simplest way to give them back softness and shine.

01

Softness and shine

A light layer smooths the surface of each strand, so your hair looks glossy and feels soft.

02

Holds in moisture

Used after you moisturize, oil helps lock it in, so your ends stay soft longer.

03

Less snagging

Smoother strands slide past each other, so combing and styling pull less.

Why light matters

Most oils were made for curls. On straightened hair they sit heavy and look greasy. Dream Hair Oil is made light, so you get the softness and shine without the weight.

How to use it

  1. Wet or dry hair. Use it after washing, or any day your ends feel dry.
  2. Spritz 2 to 3 times on your mid-lengths and ends.
  3. Keep it off your scalp and roots. A little goes a long way.

Why STRAND

Made by a woman who was tired of the guesswork and disappointing results.

I've relaxed my hair since I was about ten. Even with the budget to try everything, I couldn't find simple, user-friendly products built for my haircare in between.
