Know your hair

What chemical straightening does to your hair

Relaxers change your hair's inner bonds permanently. The straightened part stays straight. New growth comes in with your natural texture.

So every strand carries two textures. Gentle care where they meet keeps your style going longer.

Keratin and smoothing treatments reshape those bonds for a while. The curl softens for a few months, then slowly comes back.

Either way, straightened hair loves light moisture. Heavy products sit on top and flatten your style.

From the experts we talked toCare for chemically straightened hair has been overlooked for years. Your choice to straighten deserves products made for it. That's what we're building.
