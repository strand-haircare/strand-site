Grease won't calm an itchy scalp.

The truth: grease and heavy oils coat your scalp without clearing the flakes and buildup underneath. So the itch is back by midweek, and your roots look heavy too.

Do this instead

Give your scalp its own step. On wash day, massage your scalp before you shampoo to loosen flakes and buildup. Between washes, use something light on your scalp, not your lengths.
