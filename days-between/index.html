Your first week after a touch-up.

Your hair looks and feels great for a few days. Then it's up to you. Here's what we've learned from chemists, stylists and women who chemically straighten, day by day. No guesswork.

For relaxed, texlaxed, keratin and smoothed hair · 4 minute read

Days 1 to 2

Let it settle.

  • Keep it simple. Your hair and scalp just had a big day. Skip extra products and let your style set.
  • If your scalp feels sensitive, give it a break from scratching and heavy products.
  • Wrap it at night with a silk or satin scarf or bonnet. It holds your shape and cuts friction while you sleep.
Days 3 to 4

Keep it light.

  • This is usually when hair starts to feel dry or flat. Reach for light moisture on your ends, not your roots. A few drops worked through the lengths goes a long way.
  • Heavy creams and greases sit on top of straightened hair and flatten your style. Less is more.
  • Touching up with a flat iron? Low heat, a heat protectant, and only the pieces that need it.
Days 5 to 7

When the roots come back.

  • Sweat, humidity and new growth start to show at the root. That's normal. It's two textures meeting on one strand.
  • Go easy where they meet. Comb from the ends up with a wide-tooth comb, and skip tight ponytails right at the root line.
  • A soft, low bun or a wrap style gives your hair a rest without tension.
  • Scalp feeling itchy before wash day? Use your fingertips, not your nails, and keep products light at the root.
What a chemist told us

The cosmetic chemist on our team told us relaxed hair can grow long when it's cared for, but nobody explains how. Most relaxer kits come with no aftercare guidance at all. That's what The Days Between is for.

From women in the same boat

Two women we talked to this week keep it simple: a few drops of a light oil on their ends between touch-ups. Got a tip that works for you? Tell us when you join, and we'll share the best ones here, no names.
