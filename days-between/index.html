This week's drop
Drop 1 · Tip

Flat by day 3? Try less.

Straightened hair holds less moisture inside the strand, so heavy creams and greases sit on top. That's what flattens your style fast.

Light layers win. A few drops on your ends, not your roots.

Try it this week

Cut your usual amount in half. Ends only. See if day 3 looks different.
