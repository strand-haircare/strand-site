Drop 1 · Myth · Week of Nov 9, 2026

Myth: more product, more moisture.

The truth: heavy creams and greases mostly sit on top of chemically straightened hair. They weigh it down, and that's often why your style looks flat by day 3.

Do this instead

A few drops of something light, on your ends only. This week, try cutting your usual amount in half and see if day 3 looks different.
