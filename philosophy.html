Our routine philosophy

Simple. Clean. Effective.

Caring for chemically straightened hair shouldn't take a shelf of products, a PhD in hair, or your whole weekend. We're here to give you your time back. Here's what guides everything we make.

01

Simple

A short routine that gives you your time back. You'll always know what to use, when, and why.

  • A few products, not fifteen
  • Minutes, not hours
  • A routine card that tells you exactly what to do
  • Made to fit the weeks between touch-ups
02

Clean

Short ingredient lists, nothing hidden, and light formulas that don't weigh your hair down.

  • Every ingredient listed
  • Light textures made for straightened hair
  • Only what your hair needs
03

Effective

Made for chemically straightened hair, not borrowed from other routines.

  • Built for straightened lengths and new growth
  • Developed with input from stylists, chemists, and clients
